- **Outliers after the change:** 2 of the 8 runs were slow (11.6s and 15.9s). The first batch of three after the change was mixed (average 11.7s), then five runs of the same question settled around 7.5s. Model latency varies, so don't expect a steady 7.5s.
- **Why the saving is less than half:** most of the time is spent generating the proposal. The prediction is a small part of the output, so the saving is mostly one fewer round trip and one fewer prompt to process.

**What changed**
- **One call:** `services/experiment_designer.py` now asks Sonnet for one JSON object with `proposal` and `prediction`. Both are still separate validated Pydantic objects, and the response shape is the same as before.
- **Metric check:** a wrong prediction metric now fails validation inside the call, so it gets the same retry-once-then-502 treatment as any invalid output. Before, it failed after the call with no retry.
- **Safety:** the safety check still runs on the generated proposal before anything is returned.
- **Removed:** `services/predictor.py`, since the combined call replaced it. I didn't touch Jev, the client, the safety rules or the route.
- **Tests:** 57 pass, including one that confirms there's exactly one reasoning call and one that checks the wrong-metric retry.
