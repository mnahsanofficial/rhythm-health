### Proposed design direction

- **Type:** Keep Fraunces, Instrument Sans, and Geist Mono; they already suit the subject. Make the question, current day, and result more dominant, and reduce competition from small labels and repeated card headings. Keep line lengths comfortable on wide screens.
- **Colour:** Keep oat, paper, ink, moss, night, and sun. Use night and moss consistently to distinguish baseline from test days, and reserve sun for rhythm or time markers. Reduce the repeated pale-green card treatment.
- **Spacing and components:** Keep the mobile-first gutters and tap targets. Make the daily experiment and its progress the dashboard’s center. Use fewer, more varied surfaces: a clear timeline, a compact daily check-in, and a distinct forecast/result comparison.
- **Motion:** Keep Motion for React, already in the project. Its current docs support named transitions and `AnimatePresence`; `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` disables transform/layout animation for people who prefer reduced motion, while `useReducedMotion` allows a component-specific alternative. [Transitions](https://motion.dev/docs/react-transitions) · [AnimatePresence](https://motion.dev/docs/react-animate-presence) · [Accessibility](https://motion.dev/docs/react-accessibility)

Proposed tokens:

| Token | Duration and easing | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `motion-press` | 120ms, ease-out | A tap or choice responds immediately. |
| `motion-shift` | 220ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | Selected state, progress marker, or small content change. |
| `motion-settle` | 360ms, a damped spring | A day marker or choice settles into its new state. |
| `motion-reveal` | 480ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | A meaningful screen-state reveal, not every section. |
| `motion-count` | 700ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | A result number counts to its final value once. |

Motion should always answer an action or show a state change. Animate transform and opacity only, never hold up input, and preserve all information when motion is reduced.

### Everyday loop and memorable moments

1. **Check-in:** A selected rating gives immediate press and selection feedback. After submission, the day marker visibly lands as complete, with a clear confirmation.
2. **Progress:** Counts move once to their final values; the progress line fills with `scaleX`; past, current, and future days remain visually distinct. A completed day gets a clear marker.
3. **Onboarding:** Keep the directional step slide and moving progress indicator; make choice feedback more legible. The summary gathers the answers into the rhythm view.
4. **Loading:** Keep the proposal-shaped skeleton. Show the path from question to plan to forecast as an explanatory visual, without implying fabricated progress.
5. **Page transitions:** Use a short, low-distance transition that preserves context and leaves controls usable immediately.

The two memorable moments should be:

- **Onboarding summary:** the user’s sleep rhythm and answers come together as one personal picture.
- **Lab report:** the predicted range and the observed result reveal side by side, with an honest explanation of what the comparison means.
